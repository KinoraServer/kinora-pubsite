---
title: Automatic audio and subtitles
description: Kinora remembers the audio and subtitle tracks you pick for a show, like Japanese audio with English subtitles, and applies them to every episode.
---

When you change the audio or subtitle track in a show, Kinora remembers that choice and applies it to the next episodes. Watching an anime in Japanese with full English subtitles? Pick them once, and every episode starts that way, even if track order changes between files.

It works in the web player and the Android TV app. It's a module, so admins can turn it off in **Admin → Modules**.
